About me

I'm an Information Security student at the University of South Florida, graduating December 2026, with a growing specialization in data analysis and network security. Most of my recent hands-on work has come through The Global Career Accelerator, where I analyze real-world datasets for outside organizations — building Tableau and Excel dashboards, hunting for trends, and turning raw numbers into decisions a team can act on.

Outside of that internship, I've built Python tools for sales tracking, grade analysis, and general data processing, and I run Linux (Ubuntu) day-to-day so I'm comfortable in the environments where security work actually happens.

My goal after graduation is to move into a SOC analyst role, where I can put that mix of data fluency and security fundamentals to work defending networks in real time. This portfolio collects the coursework, internship projects, and reflections that are getting me there — I'll be adding to it throughout the semester.
